# Sparks & Inheritance ​

Sparks from an Uma’s Parents and Grandparents provide Stats, Aptitudes, and Skills. Sparks activate three times during a Career: At the start of the Career, and the two Inspiration Events.

Sparks are divided into Blue, Pink, Green, and White.

[image: An example Super Creek’s Sparks.]
An example Super Creek’s Sparks.

## Blue Sparks ​

Blue Sparks increase Stats at the start of a Career and during Inspiration events. Blue Sparks are usually used to increase Stats that are not prioritized during a Career. The Blue Spark can be randomly chosen from any Stat, and the star odds are determined by Stat thresholds of 600 and 1100.

INFO

Use an equal 20% selection estimate for each Stat. Hakuraku's small measured deviations do not
establish a Stat-weighted formula; see [Blue generation](hakuraku-spark-generation.md#blue-generation).

| Blue Spark Chance | 1★ | 2★ | 3★ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <600 | 90% | 10% | 0% |
| 600-1099 | 50% | 45% | 5% |
| 1100+ | 20% | 70% | 10% |

It is recommended to pursue Blue Sparks of necessary Stats that are not prioritized during the Career.

## Pink Sparks ​

Pink Sparks affect your Uma's Aptitudes. A parent’s Pink spark is randomly chosen between each of their A or S aptitudes, with each aptitude having an equal chance of being chosen.

| Pink Spark Chance | 1★ | 2★ | 3★ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A or S Aptitude | 20% | 70% | 10% |

Pink Sparks can also increase an Uma’s Aptitudes before the start of the run, up to a maximum of 4 steps.

INFO

This cannot increase an Uma’s Aptitude to S.

Classic and Senior Year Inspirations are required to raise Aptitude further.

## Green Sparks ​

Green Sparks are unlocked at Star Unlock Level 3 or above. A Parent’s Inherited Unique becomes available to buy at the start of a Career, whereas a Grandparent’s Inherited Unique requires an Inspiration activation. Inheritance grants access and hint discounts, not an automatic Skill purchase.

Green Spark star quality follows the measured White score bands below. The UE band for Green is a community extrapolation, not a measured result. [1]

## White Sparks ​

There are 3 types of White Sparks: Race, Scenario, and Skill sparks.

Race Sparks provide either two Stat bonuses or a Stat bonus and a Skill, Scenario Sparks provide two Stat bonuses, and Skill Sparks provide the corresponding Skill. Skill priorities depend on running style, track, and available sources. The source recommends Uma Stan for backliners and Groundwork for Front Runners as examples. White Sparks can make Skills available without using a Support Card that supplies them.

- Skill sparks can be generated by learning the Skill during a Career, and only the White version of the Skill can be generated.
- Race sparks are generated from winning G1 races during the Career. Winning the same race multiple times does not increase the chance of generating the spark.
- Scenario Spark conditions depend on the scenario. The source lists winning the URA Finale Finals, playing a Unity Race, and winning the TS Climax. This is not a complete list of scenarios; see [Our Grand Concert](gametora-our-grand-concert.md) for its separate Scenario Spark.
The probability to generate any given White Spark is rolled independently, i.e., there is no maximum to the amount of White Sparks that an Uma can generate.

Let `n` count the Parents and Grandparents carrying the target Skill Spark, up to six.
The estimates below use `base × 1.1^n` and are rounded to two decimal places.

| Ancestors carrying the Skill Spark | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal White Skill | 20% | 22% | 24.20% | 26.62% | 29.28% | 32.21% | 35.43% |
| ◎ Skill | 25% | 27.50% | 30.25% | 33.28% | 36.60% | 40.26% | 44.29% |
| Gold Skill | 40% | 44% | 48.40% | 53.24% | 58.56% | 64.42% | 70.86% |

This is an empirical fit to Skill Spark measurements, not a recovered server formula.
Buying the highest form gives one generation roll for the family's basic White Spark.
An actual ◎ upgrade must exist; this row does not apply to every ordinary White Skill.
Do not extend this table to Race or Scenario Spark generation without separate evidence.

## Inspiration Events ​

Inspiration Events occur at the beginning of April in Classic and Senior Years.

- Blue Sparks will still increase Stats, but by a random amount.1* Sparks will roll between 1-10, 2* 1-16, and 3* 1-28. Higher stars will also have better odds to roll higher Stats.
- Pink Sparks inherited at A rank Aptitude will raise it to S rank.Pink Sparks have a hidden innate value when obtained during Inspiration Events. Because of this, it is possible to increase Aptitude by 2 steps with one Pink Spark.
- Parents have a higher chance to proc Sparks than Grandparents.
- The source interprets a Gold Inspiration animation as a roll that activated only because a Spark was 3★. This animation interpretation is not established by the curated probability measurements; do not use it to infer an additional bonus.

## Affinity ​

A major aspect to consider when making Parents is Affinity.

In-game, total Affinity is displayed through shapes:

◎ Great Affinity (151 points or more)

〇 Good Affinity (51 through 150 points)

△ Poor Affinity (50 points or less)

These boundaries follow [GameTora's calculator](aptitude-inheritance.md#displayed-compatibility).

The base Affinity is calculated by a predetermined Affinity value between different Umas. The Affinity of a Parent is then increased by 3 points for each shared G1 race win with the other Parent and with their own Grandparents (G2/G3 wins and epithets no longer count, since the Second Anniversary Balance Patch).

When calculating odds of inheriting Sparks, the formula below is used. Each Parent and Grandparent has individual Affinities, and will calculate separately during Inspiration Events. This is why Grandparents will often have much worse Inspirations than Parents.

### Spark inheritance formula

For each source, estimate `p = min(1, base × (1 + individual Affinity / 100))` per
Inspiration Event. For two independent events, the chance of at least one activation is
`1 - (1 - p)^2`. Use each ancestor's individual Affinity, not the displayed total.
The two-event expression counts activation, not the chance of a particular final Aptitude
grade when more than one grade increase is needed.

### Base Inheritance Rates, based on Affinity (base rates) ​

| Spark type↓ / Base chance→ | ☆1 Rate | ☆2 Rate | ☆3 Rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blue Stats | 70% | 80% | 90% |
| Red Spark & Aptitude whites | 1% | 3% | 5% |
| Green (Unique Skill) | 5% | 10% | 15% |
| Race | 1% | 2% | 3% |
| White Skills, Scenario , Hidden whites | 3% | 6% | 9% |

The above table is based on data from 0 affinity Umas.
