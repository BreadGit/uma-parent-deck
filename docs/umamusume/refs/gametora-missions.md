# GameTora limited missions

Source: [Global missions](https://gametora.com/umamusume/missions), retrieved 2026-10-04.
The manifest entry `en/missions/limited` supplies event windows, official mission text
and translated text containing race instance references. The cached source is
`data/raw/en__missions__limited.json`; `races.json` and `ura-races.json` in the same
directory resolve those references and their career dates.

The feed includes future events and permanent sets. Permanent sets currently use
placeholder end dates in 2050; the limited-mission import excludes those. Dates are
source schedules, not guarantees that an upcoming event will occur unchanged.
References 10003 (URA finals) and 40001 (TS Climax) have no fixed half-month entry
in the ordinary three-year calendar. Their requirements must remain visible even
though the grid cannot place them.

The Mile Championship set (event 201) requires Fuji Stakes, Swan Stakes and Mile
Championship wins. Fuji and Swan both occur in late October of Classic and Senior
year, so a single career must use different years for those wins. The mission text
also requires completed careers; winning its three named races does not by itself
complete the whole set.

The app's trainee suggestions are a planning assumption: base surface and distance
grades B or better, with one distinct required race per available half-month and
standard career goal dates reserved. They are not evidence of win probability or
of satisfying repeat wins, margins, fans, stats or other mission conditions.
