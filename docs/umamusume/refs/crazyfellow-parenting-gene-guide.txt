Scope note

This is a broad research document covering inheritance, parent farming, scenarios, and training modes. It remains useful as a reference, but mixes historical advice, later corrections, Global mechanics, and newer JP-only features. Check each section's date, server, and training mode before applying it. Project editorial corrections dated 2026-09-18 reconcile known contradictions in shared mechanics, label historical sections, and separate mode-specific evidence. Original research, opinions, and unresolved claims remain attributed to the author. Use README.md in this directory to find curated notes and source precedence. Scenario rankings and JP release discussions are historical observations, not a current Global availability or card recommendation list.

Mode distinction

This guide discusses two separate game features:

- Independent Training, which the guide usually calls Background Auto or BG auto.
- Auto-Train / おまかせ, which the guide usually calls Auto-kun.

The guide says these modes are assumed to share much of their decision logic, but that is an inference rather than proof that every mechanic is identical. Do not transfer an Auto-kun claim to Independent Training without mode-specific evidence. In particular, the guide's statement that prioritized skills make Auto-kun choose support-card "!" hints does not establish that Independent Training does the same. Independent Training support-hint prioritization remains unverified.

Chapter 1: Compatibility & Inheritance effects

When starting a run, both your parents' overall compatibility score will be displayed using the ◎ / 〇 / △ symbols.

Overall Compatibility
◎ Great compatibility (151 points or more)
〇 Good compatibility (51 through 150 points)
△ Poor compatibility (50 points or less)

Project verification (2026-09-18): these exact boundaries match GameTora's compatibility calculator. Its symbol expression tests total > 150, then total > 50. Source https://gametora.com/_next/static/chunks/2464-2e90db37675bc47e.js, linked by https://gametora.com/umamusume/compatibility. The overall tier is separate from the individual affinity used for spark activation.


This overall compatibility is calculated based on the relationship of all parents & grandparents with your target horse, added with the total number of overlapping races that each of them has run.


However, the exact compatibility numbers are hidden in the game and you will need to use external tools to figure out the number if you want to min max it.


**August 2026 update JP version only | 5.5 Anniversary update
A new QOL has been added to the JP version. Compatibility numbers are now displayed and individual breakdowns can be shown. I'm not going to retroactively tweak the rest of my guide to accommodate this visibility update, so all you get will be this note.
While the game displays a single cumulative symbol (◎/〇/△), the underlying math is actually the sum of several Individual Compatibility links.
Individual Compatibility is the hidden point value assigned to a specific parent or grandparent in your lineage. The "Overall" symbol is simply the final result of these separate individual links being added together. This will be visited in greater detail in later chapters.
Why it matters: You might see a ◎  "Overall" rating and think the parents are a perfect match,but it may just be the sum of poor compatibilities, for example, 50 + 25 + 25 + 50 + 25 + 25 = 200. Even that total can include weak individual relationships. You should definitely worry if you do not see ◎ before starting a run.
Ideally you’d want your overall compatibility above 300, optimally around 500.


There is no bonus compatibility from running any same race a second time. For example, winning Arima Kinen in Classic or Senior contributes the same compatibility as winning it once.


PS: The effects of compatibility and inheritance genes stop at the grandparent level. There is no hidden mechanic for having a parent Uma Musume with 100 generations behind her.
How can you control these points?
*24 June 2026. Global is now using the updated compatibility system.
The Current System (JP Post-2.0 anniversary / Grand Masters and beyond scenarios)
The compatibility system has been revamped to only count racing G1 races.
* G1 Only: Only overlapping G1 wins now grant bonus compatibility.
* Simplified Gains: G2/G3 races and Title bonuses (Triple Crown/Tiara) no longer contribute.
* Increased Value: G1 races now grant +3 points each (previously +1), and the base compatibility for Umas has been boosted across the board.
* Parent to Parent overlapping races now count towards compatibility.
General Parenting Strategy
You should always run the maximum number of G1s available to you, as achieving ◎ compatibility is now significantly more straightforward than before. This also means that you should modify your aptitudes to be able to race in mile and dirt races if possible.


As a rule of thumb, overlap as many G1s as possible for a respectable compatibility bonus. The ideal race count is between 22 to 26 when including dirt rotations. With enough overlapping races, it is entirely possible to achieve ◎ compatibility using only one side of the parentage line. Sample Urara below with ◎ from just ONE side.


Recommendations:
Ever since the 2.0 anniversary compatibility overhaul, I think it is overkill to place too much emphasis on Uma to Uma compatibility. Bonus races already account for the bulk of your compatibility.

Just race every G1 race, 20~ if you do turf only, 26+ with dirt. Take care about mixing Tiara and Crown race rotation Umas as this means less races overlap and reduces compatibility from the ideal max.

The ideal way to bridge the compatibility loss with Tiara vs Triple Crown rotations is by enabling dirt racing via raising dirt aptitudes, or alternatively on modern scenarios that do not have objective races.

Global Section (URA to GL scenario) - Legacy Compatibility
** This legacy compatibility system is currently obsolete for all different server region versions of the game as of 24 June 2026
Apparently Cygames (Global) decided to push the compatibility system earlier and it is no longer delayed till the 2.0 Anniversary patch (GM scenario)

Historical Global guidance before 24 June 2026. The legacy system counted G2 and G3 wins. Those wins no longer provide compatibility bonuses. Existing parents retain their race records, but current compatibility uses the updated rules above.

Under that historical system, trainers could stack 60+ overlapping G1/G2/G3 wins in MANT for high compatibility. The legacy explanation and title table below describe those old rules only.
Raising Compatibility
In the legacy compatibility system, bonus compatibility is created via stacking overlapping races across your parenting lineage with bonuses from assembling titles/epithets.
* Races: For every identical race win (G1, G2, or G3) shared between a parent/grandparent and the target horse, you gain +1 compatibility point.
* Title Bonuses: Obtaining specific title/epithets (e.g., Triple Crown or Triple Tiara) grants an additional +1 point per title.
* Individual Links: These points are calculated individually for every connection in your family tree (Left side vs. Right side).
*Reminder that there is no bonus compatibility from running any same race a second time.
It is entirely possible that you may build super parents by running and winning the same 63 races for both parents A & B for a bonus of over 150 points to compatibility.

自主トレ育成 / Background auto / Independant training
The author records the JP launch of background auto on 30 June 2026 and its announced Global rollout in early July 2026. Global calls this mode Independent Training.


Screenshots will be from the JP version unless someone who plays global wants to feed me the relevant screenshots.


I originally thought a guide wasn't necessary for background auto since it's so straightforward, but I realised people kept asking the same questions or just didn't understand how it works so… Here I am.


What is this new function?
Background auto is a new function that allows you to schedule an automated run in the background. This is an updated QOL for the auto-kun function released for JP server but it is a completely fresh new feature for Global server.


What this does is it runs auto training in the background over a fixed time of 50 minutes per run. Unlike auto-kun who can be summoned to take the wheel in certain scenarios, you are allowed to use background auto in EVERY scenario without restrictions.


What are the pros and cons of using normal auto-kun or Background auto?
Both of them are assumed to use the same auto-logic and will suck for certain scenarios with heavy mechanics.


Auto-kun:
Pros
* Faster than BG auto, as fast as 15mins for URA, 25-35 mins on ramen scenario.
* You can win races at better odds especially when parent farming with large consecutive race stretches.
Cons
* Requires your device to be active for the duration of the run.
* Requires manual intervention if you enabled certain options such as manual inheritance picking (Umaplan users only) or pause during x event.


Background auto:
Pros
* Can be done in the background and you can actually watch the Uma Musume bond stories, live theatre and other game events you normally wouldn’t be able to do while playing.
* Can never fail the run. Objective races with a placement requirement are automatically won; participation-only objectives still roll the ordinary race odds.
* Guaranteed wins against difficult opponents during objective races, but has a dual-edged edge problem, elaborated below..
Cons
* Winning races is chance-based. Your aptitude (ranked A to G) affects win probability — higher is better. However, certain Umas can still win races reliably even with low (C or D) aptitudes. Since background auto tends to pick up random race losses, using auto-kun may give much better overall compatibility
* All runs are fixed at 50 minutes and cannot be interrupted nor influenced. If you somehow decide u want to play the game manually, you cant intervene and will need to wait or delete the run.


Special race win chance mechanics
Races in this mode use a completely different mechanic to determine if you win the race or not.


1. You can bypass races with overpowered opponents via rolling the race winning odds. For example - Umas like Narita Brian, Sakura Laurel, Verxina, or Curren Bouquet’dor who normally face Gorilla’s, bears or extinction tier threats during their respective races will have no problem can still come in 1st as they will be rolling a 110% chance to win these races.
2. Objective races - You will only auto win all objective races that if failed, will end the run. For example, this doesn’t mean u’ll win Urara’s objective race for Arima Kinen, if you have her default aptitudes - as this objective does not fail her run.
3. Non objective races
Your stats will not be factored into the odds of winning races. Instead, the win chances of entering races are purely percentage-based, following the same odds as the Green Ticket/Inheritance-only Umas. If you are interested to learn more about this system, it has its own section later in the guide found here.


Your odds of winning a race begins at 110% if you have A/A distance and ground aptitude for the corresponding race you are entering. (Cygames provided this number)
S aptitude has been known to function the same as A aptitude, also beginning at 110%


The odds are lowered for each surface or distance aptitude below A, with a streak penalty starting at the third consecutive race. A/A absorbs that first penalty because its raw score falls from 110% to 105%, still above the 100% cap. Cygames did not provide any details on the exact odds it will be lowered by. However, our trusty lad Shoppo_ura has spent the time investigating the odds.


Shoppo’s tweet here https://x.com/shoppo_ura/status/2071769445605999036?s=20 with his table of results.

Ideally if you don’t wish to gamble on 50% odds, the better choice would be the 3rd or 4th option for Anshinzawa’s event. However this cannot be conveyed to auto-kun as no skills are attached to these options.

2024’s Scenarios Foreword:
2024 Scenarios (UAF / GFF / Mecha) The three scenarios launched in 2024 significantly changed the inheritance meta. Unlike previous scenarios, this set does not generate "Big" Scenario Genes (which traditionally grant up to +30/+30 stats). Instead, they produce scenario-specific genes:
* UAF: Generates 3 unique genes with a mix of stats.
* GFF & Mecha: Generate 5 unique genes (one for each individual stat).
* These Mini scenario genes are the only genes that can directly give Skill points.


If you inherit at least 3 of the corresponding scenario’s genes mid run, they will grant you a new unique skill, unique to each scenario. PS: These skills are underwhelming and not worth the effort of target-farming the genes. Crucially, these special skills cannot be passed down as genes themselves.


Cygames has also changed the scenario launch schedule here, future scenarios now will come out every 4 months instead of 6.

Stat Uncap Mechanics
(This section applies to scenarios with stat caps above 1200. Global Our Grand Concert has such caps; older scenario discussions may not.)

Stat Uncap refers to the increase in maximum stat limits gained by inheriting certain genes.

Stat uncaps are affected by the quality of your blue genes during both the initial and mid-run inheritance stages. In the middle of a run, inheriting a green gene also increases the stat cap based on bonuses from the source Uma Musume. For example, Seiun's "Angling" uncaps Stamina and Intelligence following her Uma’s bonus in Stamina & Int , while Kitasan Black's "Wasshoi" uncaps Speed and Stamina. The precise amount of the stat uncap is currently unknown, but not a significant number (usually single digits).


Update: You can view which stat will be increased by clicking on their Green Genes. Agnes digital’s unique raises speed/stam/power (int is not raised even though she also has bonus there)

Chapter 4: Gene generation mechanics & farming strategies for the different grades of genes
Red Genes ( Strategy, Ground ability, Distance)


The red gene can only be randomly generated from aptitudes the Uma Musume has scored A in; Dirt/turf, distance and strategy.
So if you wish to target certain red genes, you will need to raise the target aptitude to an A grade or above while not raising any unwanted aptitude to A.


Example 1: Mayano Topgun has B grade running strategies in Sashi/Betweener & Oikomi/Chaser, these last 2 aptitudes will never be picked for inheritance unless you raise it to A grade.


Example 2: El condor pasa has B grade in Dirt tracks, and will not generate the Dirt gene unless it has been boosted to A or above.


Fun facts: At the initial launch of the game, the only way to initially obtain dirt genes was to roll a dirt gene on Haru Urara since she was the only Umnya to have dirt at A and then use her to pass the dirt gene down to another parent.
Example of a failed/bad red inheritance


Using Symboli Rudolph as an example, she is generally sought after as a strong stallion for breeding parent for 先行/差し/追込 Leader/betweener/chaser mainly because of her unique skill.


Her unique skill activates if she overtakes 3 other horses during the last leg.
However, her unique skill will be useless when paired with Runner / 逃げ horses since they won't be doing any overtaking. Therefore, you would normally not want her to inherit nor pass down the Runner red gene since it is basically useless for her offspring.

But mistakes can happen..




So how did this happen?
Symboli Rudolph’s base Runner / 逃げ aptitude is B so it would normally not be generated.
My Mayano had a red Nige gene, causing Rudolf's 逃げ aptitude to grow from B -> A & therefore made Rudolph eligible for the gene generation.

What should you actually do?
Given the luxury of choice, do not pair your target girl with parents that may lead to a generation of conflicting strategies. Do not get tricked into pairing conflicting strategies into parenting lineages even if they have the matching red gene ie. 6 Mid gene but unusable white genes.

Basic information about skill genes:


You can only generate a skill gene if you learnt the skill during the run. Even if all 6 of your parents/grandparents have the skill, but you did not pick the skill, it will not be generated / passed down.


You may only generate the basic version of any skill, no Gold version can ever be passed down. However, it is still advisable to buy gold skills if you really want to pass down that specific skill, the why will be explained further below.


Take note that unique skills (green) that have been inherited cannot be passed down as a white gene. Unless you are buying it for a very specific reason, to help win races mid run, it ultimately is a waste of skill points during a parent farm run.


Special note: If you inherit a skill which you have already learnt during an inheritance event, that skill's hint will be converted into stats.
I have no data as to which stat and what amount will be given in place of the hint but from experience it is usually a small value between 1-9.


Example situation: You have already learnt 良バ場 ○ and receive an inheritance for 良バ場. Even though you can still learn 良バ場◎, you will be given stats instead of additional hint discounts since it’s treated as a skill that you’ve already bought.

However do take note of another aspect of skill hint mechanics:
Example 2: You have already learnt 良バ場 ○, ! skill hints or end of race hints will never proc this skill for additional hints. The only way to gain additional hints for it now is to trigger the hint from a unique event with choices or a card event.

After a white skill spark generates, its star quality depends on final rank score. The 3★ estimate is 5% at scores 6,500-17,499, 10% at 17,500-28,799, and approximately 12.5% at 28,800+. The low and UE bands have different evidence limits; see hakuraku-spark-generation.md.
Race genes


Winning G1 races in first place allows you to generate race genes, which provide different bonuses, such as stats, skills, or both.
Certain G1 races also grant skill hints when inherited. For example, the Queen Elizabeth Cup race gene grants both stats and the non-400m skill 非根幹距離.




Aoharu update: Aoharu scenario gene is a bit special.
The Aoharu Scenario gene can still appear even if you
1. fail your Aoharu races.
2. Fail to complete the final URA portion of the run.


Regarding the number of race factors generated at the end of the run, this is tied to the number of GI races won and mentioned during a magazine interview with the development staff.


But whether they meant the total number of race factors OR if the particular G1 factor only appears if the G1 was run is unknown since Japanese is a very fickle language and could mean either. I’d assume they are just being vague and the race gene generation chances depend purely on luck (data below).

 Acquisition Conditions for Stat Genes:
   * The new awakening genes for Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, and Wisdom are obtained by "successfully completing 12 or more trainings" of each respective stat.
   * Only "successful" Training counts are considered; failed training turns do not contribute.
   * Simultaneously acquiring three types of stat genes within 1 training run due to the limited number of turns is difficult, and realistically, aiming for two types is more feasible than three. Less or none if you are racing heavily.
   * In auto-training, focusing on two types of stats makes it easier to obtain the corresponding stat genes.
Victory Conditions:
   * To obtain a directional gene (left or right turns) or seasonal (spring, summer, autumn, winter), you need to win at least 6 races that meet the specific condition.
   * Summer white only needs 5 additional races since the Debut race is ran in summer
   * This applies regardless of whether the race is a G1, OP, or any other type.
   * Total number of wins is irrelevant, it is about the number of wins under each specific condition.
Seasonal Genes:
   * Spring, autumn, and winter genes are relatively easy to obtain due to the presence of fixed scenario races.
   * The summer factor is more challenging, as it requires careful planning of race schedules.
   * To get the summer awakening gene, you need to win 5 summer races outside of the debut race since the debut race is always in summer. This may be difficult to fit into a training schedule.
________________


Summer Race Considerations:
   * When aiming for the summer factor, consider including OP races in the junior season, as they tend to have lower chances of failure.
   * Other summer races include: JDD, Ibis SDx2, Late July through early September junior races, early September Classic/Senior races, and summer training camp races.
Directional Genes:
   * If your umnya can run both dirt and turf races, or all distances on turf, you can likely obtain the left and right turn genes by participating in each G1 race once.
   * If the horse has limitations, you might need to run additional races.
Key takeaway:
   * The key to getting the seasonal and directional awakening genes is to win at least six races that meet the specific conditions. Summer races are the most difficult to obtain.

            1. Strategic Gene Prioritisation


Focus on the chance of generating the Red Genes first. These genes are the most crucial for upcoming competitive events (Champions Meeting, League of Heroes). For example, targeting for Turf inheritance can enable Smart Falcon to run turf events, or Mid-distance genes can boost Maruzensky's Mid-red proc rate (since she starts off at B Mid).


Prioritisation Order for Red Genes: When farming Red Genes, always prioritise in this order: Distance > Ground Type > Strategy. Making your distance aptitude S is always the most critical for pvp events.


The "S" Distance Advantage:
Achieving "S" rank in Distance is vital for competitive monthly race events. Without it, your Umamusume will be at a significant speed disadvantage against nearly every other opponent who had the foresight to prepare "S" Distance umas.


            2. Plan your support card deck to push desired stats to the highest possible grade.


The "Chicken and Egg" Problem with Blue Genes:
While it's ideal to farm Blue Genes for stats which your deck lacks, this can be challenging if you don't already have strong support cards for those specific stats. I’d figure most players initially aim for 9-star blues as a target for their first parent.
This means rolling a 3 star blue grandparent and then pairing it with a second uma with a 3 star blue, usually from rental Uma or another strong internal parent

The current favourite blue gene to have for Design Your Island scenario is Guts., Speed/power for Onsen scenario.
For global, it will be Speed/Stamina/Power for a very long time, especially under URA/Aoharu/MANT


            3. White Genes (Skills)
Support cards: Plan your support cards to acquire relevant skills.
Internal parent / Rentals: You want to make parents or use rentals to hunt for skills normally unobtainable in your deck.


            4. Compatibility
Make sure your parent route has good compatibility with the Uma you wanted to train. Try to pair Classic crown racers with Classic crown, Tiara with Tiara routes. If you need to, use dirt races to bridge the compatibility gap.
Alternatively, LARC works as a smooth brained method to bridge the compatibility of short/mile umas with mid/long umas, Dirt & turf since the scenario naturally gives them natural turf  + Mid aptitude.
However, as the scenario is shortened and the total number of overlapping races is capped out at 16. There is a hardcapped ceiling on the max compatibility. Very low skill points compared to current scenarios.


Historical Global advice before 24 June 2026 (pre-affinity rework): prioritise ◎ (Double Circle) Compatibility when pairing Umamusume. Use the in-game compatibility checker, or gametora’s compatibility tool for numbers to plan. To maximise compatibility, plan your parents to run the same overlapping races. Only G1, G2, and G3 races contribute to affinity before the rework; "OP" races do not count.
